About this edition

The computer runs on your machine through QEMU/WebAssembly. GitHub Pages serves the files; your browser does the computing.

Browser work lasts for this session. Closing, refreshing or restarting discards your files. Guest networking is currently disabled.

Use a desktop browser with WebAssembly threads and enough memory: the emulator reserves about 2.25 GiB, plus page and image overhead.
